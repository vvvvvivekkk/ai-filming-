# 12-Week Course Syllabus — AI Filmmaking Engineer: Zero to Pro

**Format:** one 3-hour session per week + 4–6 hours of homework. Works in-person or online.
**Class size:** 8–25 students. **Prerequisites:** none — a laptop with a modern browser and 16 GB RAM is enough. A GPU is optional (cloud/browser tools cover everything).
**Final project:** a **2–4 minute AI short film** made with a documented pipeline, screened in Week 12.

> Tool prices referenced were checked 2026-11-21 — see [docs/05](../docs/05-ai-filmmaking-tools.md). Re-check before each cohort.

## Budget guidance for students

- **Minimum:** $0 (free tiers + DaVinci Resolve free). Watermarks acceptable for coursework.
- **Recommended from Week 5:** the **Starter stack ≈ $44/month (₹4,172)** for 2 months — Google AI Pro, Kling Standard, ElevenLabs Starter, Suno Pro.
- **Trainers:** ask about education/team discounts; budget a shared API account (~$20) for the Week 10 demo.

## Final project

**Brief:** a 2–4 minute short film on a theme set by the trainer (or student-chosen with approval).
**Must include:** one consistent main character, at least one camera-controlled move, a 4-layer soundtrack, graded and delivered at 1080p, credits with AI disclosure.
**Submit:** final MP4, 9:16 teaser, shot list, prompts CSV, cost log, one-page pipeline report, rights audit.
**Assessed with:** the rubric in [assignments-and-rubrics.md](assignments-and-rubrics.md#final-project-rubric).

## Assessment weighting

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Assignments 1–5 | 50% (10% each) |
| Weekly quizzes ([quizzes-with-answers.md](quizzes-with-answers.md)) | 10% |
| Final project | 35% |
| Participation & peer feedback | 5% |

## Policies to set on day one

- **AI disclosure:** every submission lists tools used and what was human vs AI.
- **Consent:** no cloning real people's faces or voices without written consent — including classmates.
- **No copyrighted characters, logos or celebrity likenesses** in coursework.
- **Keys and secrets:** never share API keys in chats or slides.
- **Accessibility:** captions on all final films.
