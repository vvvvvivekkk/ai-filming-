## Module 1 — Story & Screenwriting ([docs/01](../docs/01-story-screenwriting.md))

1. A logline should contain: A) the ending B) protagonist, goal, obstacle, stakes C) the cast list D) the budget
2. In standard screenplay format, one page is roughly: A) 10 seconds B) 1 minute C) 5 minutes D) 10 minutes
3. Which action line is most filmable? A) "She feels guilty." B) "She remembers her childhood." C) "She hides the letter under a plate and forces a smile." D) "She is conflicted."
4. A slugline tells you: A) the theme B) interior/exterior, location, time of day C) the music D) camera lens
5. "Want vs need" describes: A) budget vs schedule B) what a character pursues vs what they truly require C) two locations D) dialogue vs action
6. Why limit lip-synced dialogue in AI films? A) It's illegal B) It's the hardest and costliest thing to generate convincingly C) Audiences hate dialogue D) Scripts can't contain dialogue
7. A good short-film scope is: A) 10 characters, 8 locations B) 1–2 characters, 1–3 locations, one clear change C) a trilogy D) no characters
8. When using AI for story work, the best role for it is: A) sole author B) critic and brainstorm partner while you make the decisions C) replacing the script D) only formatting
9. A "beat" is: A) the music tempo B) the smallest unit of change in a scene C) a scene heading D) a camera move
10. A shot list should include at minimum: A) shot number, size, action, duration B) actor salaries C) festival names D) colour LUTs

**Answers:** 1-B (the core formula) · 2-B · 3-C (visible action) · 4-B · 5-B · 6-B · 7-B · 8-B (keeps creativity and authorship yours) · 9-B · 10-A
